Account and Data Deletion

Wildlife Collection by Maraverse Games · Last updated: 28 October 2026

This page explains how to delete your Wildlife Collection account and the data associated with the app, published on Google Play by Maraverse Games. You do not need to reinstall the app to ask for deletion: the email route below works without it.

1. Your Play Games sign-in account and cloud save

If you signed in with Google Play Games, the app created an account for you in Firebase Authentication (a Google service). It holds a pseudonymous user ID, your Play Games player name, the link to your Play Games profile, the dates the account was created and last used, and possibly your Google account email address (see our Privacy Policy, section 2.6). While you are signed in, the app also keeps a backup copy of your game progress in the cloud (Cloud Firestore, a Google service): your collection, coins, quests, achievements and settings, your in-game profile name if you set one, and a daily and a weekly copy of it for recovery (section 2.7 of the Privacy Policy), and your entry in the leaderboard, which other signed-in players can see: your profile name, avatar, number of cards and experience points (section 2.8). Deleting your account deletes all of it.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Wildlife Collection.
  2. Go to Settings › Account.
  3. Tap Delete cloud account and confirm with Delete.

The app first deletes your cloud save, its daily and weekly copies and your leaderboard entry, then the account, straight away. If the save cannot be deleted (for example, you are offline), the account is not deleted either and the app tells you, so you can try again. If the app asks you to sign in with Play Games once more, that is Google confirming it is you before the account can be deleted.

Or ask us by email

If you no longer have the app, or the in-app option does not work for you, email support@maraversegames.com with the subject "Account deletion request" and include your Play Games player name (shown in the app as "Connected as …", and in the Google Play Games app on your profile). If you still have the app, you can also include the data ID from Settings › Privacy. We will delete the account together with your cloud save, its copies and your leaderboard entry, and confirm by email within one month. We may ask you a question to make sure the request comes from the account's owner.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: the Firebase account — the user ID, your Play Games player name, the link to your Play Games profile, and the email address if Google provided one — and your cloud save with its daily and weekly copies, including the in-game profile name stored in them, and your leaderboard entry. They are removed from Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore straight away; any residual copies in Google's systems are removed according to Google's own deletion timelines.
  • Not affected: your game on the device. Your collection, coins, and progress stay exactly as they are; only the cloud copy is removed. To delete the on-device copy too, see section 2.
  • Not affected: your Google account and your Play Games profile. Those belong to Google, and you manage them in your Google account settings.

Uninstalling the app does not delete the account. Unless you delete it, the account, your cloud save and your leaderboard entry are kept, with no automatic expiry, so that you can recover your game on another device.

2. Your game data (on your device)

Your game progress — collection, coins, quests, achievements, streaks, and settings — is stored on your device. To delete that copy, simply uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Settings › Apps › Wildlife Collection › Storage › Clear data. If you never signed in with Play Games, we never receive a copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side. If you did, the cloud copy is not removed by uninstalling or clearing data: delete it with your account (section 1), and note that signing in again on a reinstalled app restores it.

3. Data collected by third-party services

The app sends a limited amount of pseudonymous data to third parties to run and improve the game: anonymous usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase), rewarded-ads data including the advertising identifier (Google AdMob), and crash diagnostics (Sentry). This data is not linked to your name or to your sign-in account.

Because it isn't tied to an account, the most reliable way to remove it is directly from your device:

  • Clear the app's data or uninstall it. This wipes all on-device data and resets the app's analytics identifier, so future analytics are no longer linked to your device.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings › Privacy › Ads. This stops ads data being associated with your device.

The pseudonymous data already collected is automatically deleted under each service's data-retention limits. If you would still like us to remove specific records, email support@maraversegames.com and include your in-app data ID (see below); because this data has no account attached, we will delete whatever we can identify.

You can find your in-app data ID in the game under Settings › Privacy, where Request data deletion also opens an email to us with the ID already filled in. Including it lets us locate and delete the analytics records for your installation.
